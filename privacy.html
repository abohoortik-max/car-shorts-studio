Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Car Shorts Studio handles information when a creator uses the service to authorize and publish creator-owned video content.

Information we may process

The service may process authorization information supplied by supported platforms, such as access tokens and basic account identifiers, only as needed to provide the requested publishing functionality. It may also process video files and publishing metadata selected for upload.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the authorized account, prepare requested uploads, submit content through supported APIs, and check publishing status.

Data sharing

Information is not sold. Data is shared only with the platform services necessary to perform the requested authorization and publishing actions, or when required by law.

Security

Credentials and tokens should be stored securely and should not be exposed in public code or shared with unauthorized parties.

Retention

Authorization data and local workflow files are retained only as necessary for the operation of the service or until the creator removes or revokes them.

Your choices

A creator may revoke platform authorization through the relevant platform account settings and may stop using the service at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact associated with the application's developer account.
