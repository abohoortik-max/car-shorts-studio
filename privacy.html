Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Car Shorts Studio handles information when a creator authorizes an account, prepares video content, reviews publishing settings, and sends content through supported platform APIs.

Information we process

The service may process authorization information supplied by supported platforms, including access tokens, refresh tokens, and account identifiers needed to authenticate API requests. It may also process creator-selected video files, captions, publishing settings, file names, and publishing-status information.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized creator account, prepare creator-selected content, retrieve current publishing options, submit publishing requests through supported APIs, check publishing status, and maintain the requested publishing workflow.

Storage and service providers

Depending on the configured workflow, creator video files and publishing metadata may be stored temporarily in cloud storage used for the publishing queue. Automation infrastructure may temporarily download queued media in order to send it to the destination platform. Credentials and tokens are stored separately from public source code and are not intentionally exposed in public repositories.

Data sharing

Information is not sold. Data is shared only with service providers and destination platforms necessary to perform authorization, storage, automation, and publishing functions, or when required by law.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit access to credentials and publishing data. Creators should protect their own devices and platform accounts and should revoke authorization if they believe access has been compromised.

Retention

Queued media and publishing metadata are retained only as needed to operate the publishing workflow. Successfully processed items may be moved to a completed area or removed according to the configured workflow. Authorization data may be retained while the integration remains connected and can be invalidated when authorization is revoked.

Your choices

A creator may stop using the service at any time and may revoke platform authorization using the relevant platform account settings. Revocation prevents future API use once existing authorization is no longer valid.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to the support contact associated with the application's developer account.
